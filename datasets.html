Competitor Registry

Real shops, real coordinates.

Every competitor pin in the survey workbench resolves to a named OpenStreetMap feature with a verifiable OSM reference and haversine distance from the survey center.

Footfall Anchors

Metro ridership, not guesswork.

Transit anchor volumes come from BMRCL's station-wise ridership (September 2025), obtained via RTI and published by OpenCity. The Whitefield weekend collapse — 12,150 → 1,901 riders at Sri Sathya Sai Hospital station — is visible in the raw numbers.

Listing Registry

Listings aligned to published bands.

Listing templates are aligned to published corridor rent bands (e.g., Lokazen's 2026 Indiranagar guide) and clearly labeled as representative templates — never as live broker inventory.
